**SHIKARDOS AUDIO 1.1.67** · build 223 · Android 8.0 and newer

Fixes reported by listeners:

- **USB DAC volume works again in VULAN mode.** In 1.1.66 the end of a song was sometimes mistaken for the DAC being unplugged, and the volume buttons went back to Android. The player now asks the DAC itself whether it is still connected.
- **Memory cards no longer get stuck scanning forever.** If a file doesn't answer while its tags are being read, the player stops waiting for it and finishes the scan.
- **Lyrics beyond MP3.** Lyrics are now read from FLAC, OGG, M4A and APE tags, from `.lrc` files in Windows‑1251 encoding, and from LRC stored inside tags.
- **CUE sheets find their music** even when the sheet says `album.wav` but the file next to it is `album.wv` or `album.flac`.
- **Player log:** entries are no longer lost in the shared file, and it opens cleanly in any text editor.

Installs over the previous version; settings and library are kept.
