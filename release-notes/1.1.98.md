**SHIKARDOS AUDIO 1.1.98** · build 303 · Android 8.0 and newer

- **SHIKARDOS Matrix — add-ons on demand.** Radio station logos, car-screen backgrounds and headphone presets now come as packages from the website: they download in seconds and remove with one tap, and whatever you keep works offline. The player itself is now 72 MB.
- **USB DACs that ignore volume commands** (JCALLY JM6 PRO II, TTGK CX31993 and relatives): the player does the volume itself, so these DACs keep playing.
- **OFF button in the equalizer** removes all processing with one tap.
- **DSF through a USB DAC** — no hiss at the end of the track.
- **Locked screen:** changing tracks through a USB DAC no longer pauses playback.
- **"Date added" sorting** survives a restart.
- **Swipe from recent apps:** while music plays it keeps playing; on pause the player closes completely.
- **Settings remember** where you left off.
- **Update check** also goes through the cloud mirror when the website doesn't open.
- **"Support the project — if you wish"** in Settings and About. The player is free and stays free.

Installs over the previous version; settings and library are kept.
